## CHAPTER 1: SYSTEM OVERVIEW & MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS

Welcome to the cutting edge of electronic office identity deployment. The **Cinco Identity Generator 2.5™** is an integrated corporate performance platform designed specifically for the **M-VAVE FM-1 Synthesizer Terminal**. Utilizing proprietary **4d3d3d3™ Cascading Window Architecture**, the system enables office workers to synthesize, modulate, and entertayne with photorealistic digitized personnel in real time while maintaining the outward appearance of rigorous spreadsheet computation.

### Minimum System Requirements
* **Microprocessor:** 32-bit Dual-Core RISC Audio Processor (JieLi AC7916A) operating at 120 MHz.
* **Display Terminal:** 1.54" Active Matrix Color ST7789 Super-VGA LCD (240×240 native pixel resolution, 65,536 colors).
* **Sound Generation:** 16-bit 44.1 kHz Stereo DAC with hardware DC-blocking, peak limiting, and soft-saturation.
* **System Memory:** 56.8 KB Conventional SRAM, 302 KB Dynamic Heap Pool, 1 MB Non-Volatile SPI NOR Flash ROM.
* **Operator Inputs:** 27-key Velocity Sensitive Capacitive Matrix, 7 Precision Rotary Detent Knobs, 14 Tactile Action Buttons.
* **Workplace Environment:** Standard office cubicle, low-glare fluorescent illumination, minimum 8 oz. steaming coffee, zero active supervisory oversight.

### Flashing Hardware Terminal via WebMIDI Installer
To install or update the Cinco Identity Generator 2.5™ directly on your M-VAVE FM-1 hardware without any local toolchain:
1. Open Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on your computer.
2. Navigate to the **[Cinco Web Installer](https://mrjoshida.github.io/CINCO/installer/)**.
3. Connect your FM-1 console via USB-C.
4. Click **INSTALL CINCO 2.5 (cinco.fwsc)**.
5. The web flasher stages and writes the image over WebMIDI SysEx and restarts your console automatically.

### Initial Power-On Sequence
1. Connect the supplied USB-C Communications Cable from your desktop power supply or mainframe terminal into the rear receptacle of the FM-1 console.
2. Tap the **POWER** toggle or depress any keyboard key.
3. The display will illuminate with the **Cinco Identity Generator 2.5** splash banner while the internal speech synthesizer vocalizes the corporate morning greeting:
   > *"Good morning, Paul."*
4. The system will settle into the primary desktop workspace with **Celery Man** loaded into active memory.

## CHAPTER 4: THE IDENTITY ROSTER (AVAILABLE PERSONALITIES)

Turn the **`PRESET`** rotary encoder to cycle through available employee identity profiles:

### 1. Celery Man (`SRC_CELERY`)
* **Associated Sound Engine:** `ENG_ANALOG`
* **Synthesis Architecture:** Dual detuned Sawtooth + Pulse waveforms routed through a resonant 24dB ladder lowpass filter with a rapid 350 ms exponential decay envelope.
* **Musical Character:** Stiff, punchy 1980s corporate analog brass with an aggressive acoustic blip. Excellent for assertive boardroom key riffs.
* **Vocal Confirmation:** *"Celery Man."*

### 2. Oyster Smiling (`SRC_OYSTER`)
* **Associated Sound Engine:** `ENG_PHASE`
* **Synthesis Architecture:** Casio CZ-style Phase Distortion synthesis with shimmering resonant upper harmonics and 60% stereo chorus send.
* **Musical Character:** Glassy, ethereal electric keys and crystalline bells. Ideal for serene contemplative spreadsheet interludes.
* **Vocal Confirmation:** *"Oyster."*

### 3. Tayne BETA (`SRC_TAYNE`)
* **Associated Sound Engine:** `ENG_FM6`
* **Synthesis Architecture:** 6-Operator 1990s Yamaha FM configuration with snappy carrier attack and dynamic modulator index mapping.
* **Musical Character:** Slapped digital synth bass and bright FM house brass. Highly energetic.
* **Vocal Confirmation:** *"Tayne."*

### 4. Talk Mode (`SRC_TALK`)
* **Associated Sound Engine:** `ENG_FORMANT`
* **Synthesis Architecture:** Fixed-point 10-pole vocal tract formant filter modulating retro synthetic vowels.
* **Musical Character:** Melodic robotic singing voice mapped chromatically across the 27 physical piano keys.
* **Vocal Confirmation:** *"Can I entertayne you?"*

## CHAPTER 5: ADVANCED PERFORMANCE MACROS & KINETICS

The Cinco 2.5 workstation features non-modal, stackable performance controls that can be engaged simultaneously:

```
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+

```

### 1. The 4d3d3d3™ Macro (`K1` Knob)
As `K1` is rotated clockwise from `0` to `127`:
- **Audio Reaction:** Progressively drives filter resonance and stereo chorus depth into saturation.
- **Visual Reaction:** At threshold values (32, 64, 96), the active dancer window cascades across the desktop in multiples of **1x $\rightarrow$ 2x $\rightarrow$ 4x $\rightarrow$ 8x windows**, replicating authentic early graphical OS window redraw lag.
- **Threshold Warning:** Turning `K1` to maximum `127` triggers an emergency dialog: *"WARNING: 4d3d3d3 exceeded recommended OSHA flarh levels."*

### 2. Hat Wobble (`LFO` Button)
- **Operation:** Tap `LFO` to latch Hat Wobble.
- **Audio:** Injects a tempo-synchronized 6 Hz sine-wave pitch vibrato into active synthesizer voices.
- **Visuals:** Instantly overrides the current dancer with **Tayne’s Hat Wobble dance**, updating the window title to `"Tayne - Hat Wobble"`.
- **Speech:** Vocalizes *"Hat wobble."*

### 3. Flarhgunnstow (`ARP` Button)
- **Operation:** Tap `ARP` to latch Flarhgunnstow mode.
- **Audio:** Engages an ultra-fast 16th-note ratcheting arpeggiator and rhythmic amplitude slicer.
- **Visuals:** Displays **Tayne’s Flarhgunnstow flail animation**, updating the title to `"Tayne - FLARHGUNNSTOW!"`.
- **Speech:** Vocalizes *"Flarhgunnstow."*

### 4. Nude Tayne Protocol (`FX` Button)
- **Operation:** Tap `FX` to engage Nude Tayne mode.
- **Visuals:** Immediately renders a 1-second **RED SECURITY PROTOCOL ALERT** dialog (*"THIS IS NOT SUITABLE FOR WORK. ARE YOU SURE?"*), switches Tayne to pixel mosaic quantization, and displays title `"NUDE TAYNE"`.
- **Audio:** Engages the real-time **Audio Mosaic Bitcrusher**, decimating sample rate down to 4.4 kHz and masking lower bits for crunchy 4-bit lo-fi textures.
- **Speech:** Warns *"Warning: this is not suitable for work."*

### 5. Printout of Oyster Smiling (`SAVE` Button)
- **Operation:** Tap `SAVE`.
- **Visuals:** Animates a thermal computer receipt card sliding down from the screen with perforated edges, displaying an ASCII rendering of Oyster smiling `( ^ _ ^ )`.
- **Persistence:** Atomically commits all current rotary positions, active character, and octave transpose to non-volatile SPI NOR flash sector `0x0FC000`.
- **Speech:** Acknowledges *"Printout generated."*

### 6. Corporate Panic Button (`HOME` Button)
- **Operation:** Tap `HOME` when unexpected executive personnel approach your workstation.
- **Action:** Immediately cancels all performance layers (`wobble=0, flail=0, nude=0, k4d=0`), collapses all cascaded windows back to single 1x display, restores default Celery Man, and silences all arpeggiators.
- **Speech:** Fred placatingly affirms: *"Yes, Paul."*

### 1. The "Boss Key" Spreadsheet (`EDIT` Button)
* **Function:** Instantly disguises your console as a 1992 **Lotus 1-2-3 Release 3.1** spreadsheet (`CELERY_CORP_Q3.WK1`).
* **Audio Behavior:** All synthesizer audio and groove sequences continue playing unhindered, allowing the employee to compose music under the guise of accounting.

### 2. Cinco Disk Defragmenter (`SEQ` Button)
* **Function:** Switches the display to an authentic 16×6 cluster defragmentation grid (modeled after Norton SpeedDisk and MS-DOS 6.22 Defrag).
* **Cluster Key:**
  - **White Clusters:** Unallocated memory sectors.
  - **Blue Clusters:** Read-only Celery Man identity files.

  - **Flashing Amber/Red Clusters:** Live Eurodance sequencer steps pulsing on kicks, claps, and octave basslines.

* **Function:** Engages automatically when no keyboard keys or rotary knobs are manipulated for twenty (20) seconds.

* **Wake:** Touching any physical control wakes the console immediately without latency or missed musical notes.

### 4. Steaming Coffee Mug Activity Meter
* **Function:** Located at the bottom right corner of the desktop taskbar `(216, 222)`.
* **Behavior:** Renders an animated ceramic coffee mug with curling pixel steam trails. Rapid key playing rapidly drains the dark coffee level. Leaving the keyboard idle allows the mug to refill with fresh hot coffee. A red `!` alert appears when caffeine levels reach zero.
